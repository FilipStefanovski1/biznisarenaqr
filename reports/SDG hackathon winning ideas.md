# Rebuild Niva around the missing signature

SDG competitions are won by teams that name one specific user in one specific place, show who pays and why, and put a working artifact in front of the jury. Polished slides about global problems do not win, and neither do the categories judges have seen hundreds of times: AI tutors, mental-health chatbots, recycling-sorting cameras, carbon trackers and food-surplus marketplaces. Creative Shock 2026 is the first edition of the ISM competition to ask for original ideas rather than consulting cases. Its official briefing scores six criteria, puts Marketing and Finance at the centre of the 3–5 minute video, and adds "Vibe-Coding / Build Potential". That means a software-first idea grounded in Balkan evidence is the right shape. Seven ideas were scored against Niva, the team's current candidate. Niva scores **22/30**: its problem is real (about 32% of arable land abandoned), but its mechanism breaks. Under Article 37 of the Law on Ownership, leasing co-owned land needs **every** co-owner's consent, and parcels renting at about €100/ha cannot fund that paperwork. Three new ideas tie at **25/30**. The recommendation is **Udel**, which keeps Niva's satellite hook and turns the unanimity rule into the product: a platform that gets every heir, wherever they live, to sign, and so unlocks inherited land for leasing, sale and women's co-registration. The strongest clean-break alternative is **Pasoš**, a voice-first pesticide spray log and EU-residue passport that exporters pay for. With the video due in roughly 60 hours (23:59 EEST is **22:59 in Skopje**), Udel reuses everything the team has already built for Niva.

## Winners name one user, one payer and one working artifact

Creative Shock has no track record for original ideas yet. Up to 2025 it was a case competition. The 2025 winner, Universitas Gadjah Mada, and the runner-up, University of Belgrade FON, solved cases for Lithuanian social enterprises: an assistance-dog charity, a disability-employment paper workshop, a migrant-integration NGO and a rural heritage space ([UGM](https://ugm.ac.id/en/news/ugm-team-win-first-place-at-creative-shock-2025-international-competition-in-lithuania/); [FON](https://en.fon.bg.ac.rs/?p=38066)). What the UGM winners said mattered carries over to 2026. Their solutions had to fit Lithuania's local context to be "relevant and implementable," and had to be delivered "clearly, simply, and engagingly" to a mixed jury. An earlier edition included a round where teams pitched their own social business idea. Rotman Commerce won with a homelessness venture for its home city, Toronto ([Rotman Commerce](https://rotmancommerce.utoronto.ca/article/rotman-commerce-students-win-creative-shock-case-competition/)). The 2026 format is a **3–5 minute video of an original impact business idea tied to one SDG**. The online round runs Oct 5–11, the **Top 10 meet in Vilnius Nov 18–21**, and the Top 3 share a $4,000 package ([creativeshock.lt](https://creativeshock.lt/); [event PDF](https://www.hochschule-stralsund.de/storages/hs-stralsund/International/Outgoing/News__Events__Scholarships/Creative_Shock_event_description.pdf)). The team's official briefing settles what the public pages left open. The video must cover **marketing** (problem, who it is for, what is different, why customers choose it, how to reach them) and **finance** (revenue, what customers pay for and why, main costs, why it is viable and scalable). The six scoring criteria include **Vibe-Coding / Build Potential**, so a team must convince the jury it can build a compelling MVP with AI tools during the final weekend.

Hult Prize, the largest comparable circuit, shows what a mature version of that standard looks like. Every single global winner since 2022 has been a concrete product for a named, under-served user. Ecobana made affordable period products in Kenya. Banofi made banana-waste leather. Korion Health built an at-home electronic stethoscope for rural and low-income patients. Stick 'Em made chopstick STEAM kits ([EF Impact](https://impact.ef.com/stories/the-hult-prize-social-entrepreneurship/); [UMD CS](https://www.cs.umd.edu/article/2024/09/alum%E2%80%99s-heart-screening-startup-wins-1m-hult-prize); [NUS Computing](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/news/computing-students-behind-stick-em-winner-of-the-2025-hult-prize)). The 2026 winner, KAUST's Flow Guard, sells AI smart water meters to utilities. Hult's CEO said it stood out for "turning an urgent global problem into a practical, scalable business" ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/1-million-awarded-to-social-impact-student-start-up-at-2026-ef-hult-prize-302872716.html)). Hult's campus rubric spells out the checklist judges carry into the room. It asks whether the solution has been "tested with real users," whether there are **clear impact KPIs that grow with revenue**, and whether the unit economics hold ([HKUST Hult Prize](https://ec.hkust.edu.hk/node/11978)). Hult judges at MNIT Jaipur in 2026 pushed teams on exactly the gaps that sink student pitches: problem validation, revenue clarity and measurable impact ([MNIT E-Cell](https://examination.mnit.ac.in/TS/pdf/ECELL_HULTPRIZE.pdf)). Other circuits follow the same pattern. ClimateLaunchpad 2025 went to algae fuel in South Africa, electric motorcycles for Tanzanian riders and insect protein in Colombia ([Climate-KIC](https://www.climate-kic.org/?p=72249)). EIT Food's 2025 student prize went to culturally rooted legume recipes ([UNISG](https://www.unisg.it/unisg-teams-claim-the-podium-at-the-eit-food-solutions-challenge-in-brussels/)). Menu for Change funded sustainable procurement for Czech university cafeterias ([Food Manufacture](https://www.foodmanufacture.co.uk/Article/2025/02/20/gen-z-innovators-win-eu-food-innovation-challenge)).

One pattern matters for a software team. The pure-software Hult 2026 finalists, Capynova, Dihadi and CleanUp MDC, lost to hardware-plus-AI ventures ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/1-million-awarded-to-social-impact-student-start-up-at-2026-ef-hult-prize-302872716.html)). Creative Shock's build criterion rewards software, but the jury will still trust ideas that touch something physical and transactional more than ideas that only show information. The best software ideas below therefore end in a real-world object or act: a field that gets farmed, a crate with a QR passport, a stove that gets replaced. Pitch length also has a clear benchmark. Korion's winning pitch ran **4 minutes** and was built around preventable deaths and one emotional call to action ([UMD CS](https://www.cs.umd.edu/article/2024/09/alum%E2%80%99s-heart-screening-startup-wins-1m-hult-prize)). University video-pitch rules recommend a hook and team intro in the first 15 seconds, then problem, solution, customer, business model and next steps, and they treat overrunning the time limit as grounds for disqualification ([NC State Pitch to the Pack](https://pitchtothepack.textiles.ncsu.edu/pitch-video-instructions)).

## Five crowded categories and four quiet SDGs

The best quantitative evidence of where student teams cluster comes from a University of Geneva study of **1,320 SDG-related hackathons and 37,647 projects on Devpost**. Four goals dominate: **SDG 4 education (24.3%)**, SDG 9 infrastructure (21.6%), SDG 8 decent work (20.1%) and SDG 10 inequality (12%) ([Maillart et al., arXiv 2410.03286](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.03286)). At MIT RAISE 2025, SDG 3 health and SDG 4 education were the most-addressed goals ([MIT RAISE](https://raise.mit.edu/?p=916)). Five specific categories are demonstrably saturated:

- **Mental-health apps:** an estimated 10,000–20,000 exist, and only 1–3% have direct scientific evidence ([Psychotherapy.net](https://www.psychotherapy.net/interview/stephen-schueller-on-mental-health-apps)).
- **Carbon-footprint trackers:** a crowded consumer field with known retention failure, because few people want to track their footprint continuously ([Atmos](https://atmos.earth/where-are-the-good-climate-change-apps/); [AIGA Eye on Design](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/carbon-tracking-apps-are-designed-to-foster-responsibility-is-this-a-flaw/)).
- **Food-surplus marketplaces:** Too Good To Go has about 66.5 million users, and roughly ten named rivals already cover the obvious variants ([ProductMint](https://productmint.com/too-good-to-go-competitors/)).
- **Camera-based recycling sorters:** these have won hackathons repeatedly since 2017, so judges know the concept by heart ([Auckland Climathon](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/news/2017/11/01/results-inaugural-auckland-climathon.html); [U-M Hacks with Friends](https://michigan.it.umich.edu/news/2019/03/28/winning-ideas-at-hacks-with-friends-2019)).
- **AI tutors:** the most-covered SDG combined with the wrapper problem below.

Generic "AI chatbot for X" products are a sixth trap. Hackathon commentators list them among the ideas judges are tired of, and VCs ask any LLM product "what's your moat beyond the foundation model?" ([WhereUElevate](https://whereuelevate.com/blogs/why-hackathon-projects-fail); [VC Beast](https://vcbeast.com/venture-capital-glossary/ai-wrapper)). The defensible answer is local data, a named institutional user and a workflow the model sits inside.

The failure modes go beyond category choice. SSIR criticised a "Best PowerPoint Wins" culture in business-plan competitions and argued for "apprenticing with a problem" before proposing a solution ([SSIR](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/rethinking_business_plan_competitions)). SDG-washing is another recognised failure: decorating a pitch with goal icons without tying it to one of the 169 targets ([UNRIC](https://unric.org/nl/sdg-washing-bedrijven-beweren-ten-onrechte-dat-ze-zich-inzetten-voor-de-sdgs/)). Scholars of civic hackathons warn against technological solutionism, meaning an app proposed for a structural problem with no partner who controls the system ([ASEE PEER](https://peer.asee.org/engagement-in-practice-social-performance-and-harm-in-civic-hackathons)).

The quiet side of the map is less precisely measured. The Geneva study does not publish its bottom ranking. However, no top-coverage list includes **SDG 6 (water), 14, 15 (land) or 16 (institutions)**. UN data also show that the environmental goals 6, 12, 13, 14 and 15 are the least reported on in national reviews ([UN DESA VNR synthesis](https://policy.desa.un.org/sites/default/files/inline-images/eapd2023/VNRs-2020-Goals-targets.pdf)). That makes SDG 5 (gender), 6, 15 and 16 the likeliest under-crowded goals. This is an inference, not a measured ranking. One paradox matters. SDG 12 and SDG 13 are under-reported nationally, yet they attract the most saturated consumer apps. A team should pick an under-crowded goal **and** a non-consumer payer, not just an unusual goal icon.

## The open ground sits at the "first mile", where the data lives on paper

The under-tackled spaces share one structure. Well-funded incumbents serve the big buyer, and the small actor who holds the real-world data is left with paper, Viber and no budget. Globally, EUDR platforms like Meridia (now SGS's verification partner) serve multinationals ([SGS](https://www.sgs.com/en-mm/news/2026/09/sgs-selects-meridia-as-technology-partner-for-eudr-verification)). Leak-detection firms like ASTERRA and FIDO sell sensor-heavy products to larger utilities ([Geo Week News](https://www.geoweeknews.com/news/asterra-s-ai-driven-water-leak-detection-technology-is-a-major-step-toward-sustainable-water-management); [Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/fido-ai-raises-series-b-investment-to-scale-its-innovative-water-management-technology)). Procurement red-flag tools are NGO-run, rule-based and cover only nine countries ([Transparency International EU](https://transparency.eu/integrity-watch-red-flags)). LLMs changed the economics of the first mile. A model can now turn a farmer's voice note, a scanned deed or a billing CSV into the structured format a regulator or buyer needs, which was not cheap before about 2024. Combine that with **local-language data** and a payer further up the chain, and the result is defensible against the "it's just a wrapper" objection.

North Macedonia has unusually well-documented first-mile problems:

- **Air pollution:** a UNDP survey of **5,044 Skopje households** found that 85% of rural households heat with wood, only **1%** choose their heating with pollution in mind, and **40%** choose by the monthly bill ([UNDP](https://www.undp.org/north-macedonia/news/major-new-survey-household-heating-identifies-some-key-causes-skopjes-soaring-levels-pollution)). Tetovo's PM2.5 runs at about 8x the WHO guideline, and air pollution is linked to about 2,574 premature deaths a year ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/gallery/2021/4/21/in-pictures-air-pollution-in-north-macedonia)).
- **Water loss:** non-revenue water sits near **60%**. The State Audit Office found about 59% at Bitola's utility, with "difficult billing" ([State Audit Office](https://arhiva.dzr.mk/en/node/3848); [Meta.mk](https://meta.mk/en/?p=556300)).
- **Women and land:** women own **26%** of property nationally and about **5%** in rural areas, usually only after being widowed ([Global Voices](https://globalvoices.org/2025/04/30/gains-and-gaps-in-gender-equality-in-north-macedonia/); [GIZ Gender Works](https://gender-works.giz.de/?p=15527)).
- **Rural ageing:** 458 villages have fewer than 50 residents, and the 65+ share rose from 13.1% to 18.1% in a decade ([OBC Transeuropa](https://www.balcanicaucaso.org/en/cp_article/the-death-of-macedonian-village/); [UGD](https://eprints.ugd.edu.mk/37035/2/Rural%20Revitalization%20.pdf)).
- **Pesticide residues:** Bulgaria and Croatia have destroyed or withdrawn Macedonian peppers over pesticide residues, in one case methomyl at about **5x** the EU limit ([FreshPlaza](https://www.freshplaza.com/article/9871656/bulgaria-blocks-28-5-tons-of-grapes-and-peppers-over-pesticide-levels/); [HortiDaily](https://www.hortidaily.com/article/9772414/macedonian-peppers-withdrawn-from-croatian-market-due-to-contamination)).

Two cautions apply. Several of these figures date from 2017 to 2021. "No competitor found" means none surfaced in this research pass, not that a dedicated competitor search ruled them out.

The free data needed to build demos for these problems is real and weekend-friendly:

- **Open-Meteo** needs no API key and gives instant weather time series ([open-meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com/)).
- **NASA FIRMS** fire points need only a free key ([NASA FIRMS Q&A](https://appliedsciences.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/2025-05/Part%203%20FIRMS%202025%20Q&A%20Session.docx.pdf)).
- **The TED API** allows anonymous access to published EU tender notices ([TED API docs](https://docs.ted.europa.eu/api/latest/index.html)).
- **Copernicus Sentinel-2** gives the most striking before/after imagery, within a free tier reported at about 10,000 processing units a month ([ki-syndikat guide](https://www.ki-syndikat.de/tools/copernicus-data-space/)).

The safe demo practice is to pre-compute three to five hero scenes so the live pitch never depends on a quota.

## Niva's problem is real, but its mechanism breaks on Article 37

Niva's headline survives scrutiny. FAO's figure that about **32% of arable land is abandoned** was repeated at a March 2026 event with the Ministry of Agriculture and the Economic Chamber ([Economic Chamber](https://mchamber.mk/en/news/index/1023); [FAO REU](https://sfcs.fao.org/europe/news/detail/fao-continues-push-to-address-land-abandonment-in-north-macedonia/en)). Farms average under 2 ha split across 5–7 parcels, and private parcels average about **0.22 ha** ([FAO LANDNET 2022](https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/user_upload/reu/europe/documents/Events_2022/landnet13presentation/LL_.pdf)). The diaspora is about **25% of the population** ([ECONDIAS factsheet](https://diasporafordevelopment.eu/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/NM-Factsheet-v.2.pdf)). No Balkan platform for leasing private farmland surfaced. Sentinel-2 abandonment mapping is research-grade: abandoned-class accuracy runs roughly 78–87%, with fallow and extensive pasture as the weak point ([MDPI Land 2024](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-445X/13/5/586); [IJAEOG 2024](https://doaj.org/article/2325444d641c451a9a62bcd4909f9546)).

The mechanism is where Niva breaks. **Article 37 of the Law on Ownership lists leasing ("издавање на стварта во закуп") among the extraordinary acts that need the consent of all co-owners.** Without that consent, only a court can decide ([Law on Ownership, 2001 base text](https://business.gazibaba.gov.mk/media/pdf/zakonska_regulativa/pravni_raboti/sopstvenost_i_drugi_stvarni_prava/1-sopstvenost-i-drugi-stvarni-prava.pdf)). Majority decisions exist only inside state-led land consolidation ([FAO Egri](https://www.fao.org/europe/news/detail/The-first-majority-based-land-consolidation-plan-adopted-in-Egri-North-Macedonia-/en)). Heir parcels owned by emigrants are exactly the ones most likely to have several co-owners. A formal lease matters because subsidy claims are checked against the farm register and LPIS, the land-parcel register used for payments ([IPARDPA 2021 amendment](https://www.ipardpa.gov.mk/Upload/Documents/Уредба%20за%20измен%20и%20допол%20на%20уредбата%20за%20поблиските%20критериуми%2024032021.pdf)). An informal handshake lease cannot carry a subsidy claim.

The economics compound the legal problem. State tender floors are **€60–120/ha/year** ([HortiDaily](https://www.hortidaily.com/article/6019876/released-call-for-the-lease-of-land-over-3-ha-in-macedonia/)), so a 10–15% commission on a sub-hectare parcel yields single-digit euros a year. Tracing and notarising heirs costs far more than that. The demand-side hook is weak too. The tripled tax on unused agricultural land took effect in 2022 on base rates of only 0.10–0.20% ([Karanović & Partners](https://karanovicpartners.com/news/north-macedonia-latest-amendments-to-the-property-taxes-legislation)). No evidence of municipal enforcement was found, and the law has no legal definition of abandoned land ([FAO cc4778en](https://www.fao.org/3/cc4778en/cc4778en.pdf)). Every European analogue is publicly funded, including Galicia's land bank, Austria's Perspektive Landwirtschaft and Ireland's Land Mobility Service ([Urban Agenda](https://www.urbanagenda.urban-initiative.eu/sites/default/files/2026-06/Banco%20de%20Terras%20de%20Galicia%2C%20Spain.pdf); [EU CAP Network](https://eu-cap-network.ec.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2024-12/eu-cap-network-tg-gr-perspektive-landwirtschaft-initiative.pdf)). Galicia's numbers also show the fragmentation problem: about 7,000 ha spread over 19,000 plots ([Xunta](https://www.xunta.gal/gl/notas-de-prensa/-/nova/021394/medio-rural-publica-unha-nova-orde-que-facilita-tramites-arrendamento-preto-dun)).

The benchmark for every new idea is therefore three questions:

1. Does it avoid a legal choke point that it cannot control?
2. Is its revenue priced against something larger than €100/ha?
3. Does it stand without an unverified enforcement mechanism?

### Udel: get every heir to sign, from wherever they live

**Pitch:** Udel finds the family land that sits idle because seven heirs in four countries can't all sign, and gets every signature, so the land can be leased, sold or registered in a daughter's name.

**Problem evidence.** Udel starts from Niva's own finding. Leasing co-owned land legally needs unanimity, and one unreachable cousin blocks a lawful lease ([Law on Ownership](https://business.gazibaba.gov.mk/media/pdf/zakonska_regulativa/pravni_raboti/sopstvenost_i_drugi_stvarni_prava/1-sopstvenost-i-drugi-stvarni-prava.pdf)). Co-ownership lock-in is already a documented policy problem. An FAO project exists because state–private co-ownership has kept about **2,000 Pelagonija landowners** from using their own land for decades ([FAO REU](https://sfcs.fao.org/europe/news/detail/fao-project-targets-resolving-public-private-co-ownership-to-agricultural-land-and-improved-agricultural-land-management/en)). The EU/FAO consolidation programme lists "updating land ownership data" among its aims ([FAO project background](https://www.fao.org/in-action/enhancing-land-consolidation/background/en)). The gender angle is equally concrete. Rural women hold about 5% of property, partly because daughters renounce their inheritance in favour of brothers ([GIZ Gender Works](https://gender-works.giz.de/?p=15527)). Every inheritance case Udel opens is a moment to show each heir, daughters included, their statutory share before anyone signs it away. That gives the pitch a precise SDG 5.a story rather than a decorative one.

**Competitors.** No software competitor surfaced. FAO, UN Women and NGOs run awareness campaigns ([GLTN](https://gltn.net/blog/fao-land-ownership-still-lagging-women-western-balkans)). The work is otherwise done one family at a time by notaries and lawyers, who have no reason to build tooling for a low-fee, multi-country coordination task.

**Who pays.** The proposed model has four layers:

- **Free satellite "check your family's land" report:** Niva's detection layer, now used as the acquisition funnel.
- **Family case fee (about €149):** covers the heir map, share calculation, document drafts and signature tracking.
- **Referral fees:** paid by partner notaries and lawyers.
- **Lease management (about 8–10% of rent):** taken once land is leased to a young farmer.

B2G and donor contracts come later, supplying clean ownership data to consolidation projects. The key financial argument for the video is that the fee is priced against the asset, not the rent. Farmland sold at roughly **€10,000–15,000/ha** in older field data ([UKIM repository](https://repository.ukim.mk/handle/20.500.12188/7624?mode=full)), so clearing title on a family's holding is worth far more than a year's €100/ha rent. Main costs are part-time case coordinators, notary partnerships and diaspora marketing through Facebook groups, consulates, churches and community clubs. Satellite data is effectively free.

**Weekend MVP.** The build has four parts:

1. A pre-baked Sentinel-2 NDVI time series for three or four real parcels.
2. A family-tree builder that computes each heir's share and flags who must sign.
3. LLM-drafted consent, power-of-attorney and lease documents in Macedonian, Albanian, English and German.
4. A live signature tracker. Cadastre lookups are mocked.

**Wow moment.** A judge scans a QR code and becomes the last missing cousin "in Melbourne". The counter flips from 6/7 to 7/7, a lease is generated, and a waiting young farmer is matched on the map.

**Risks.**

- **Legal currency:** the analysis rests on the 2001 base text. If an amendment or the Law on Agricultural Land allows majority leasing of farmland, the core insight weakens, though Niva's original model revives.
- **Formalities:** notarisation and legalisation from abroad may still require consulates.
- **Scope of advice:** the product must stay a workflow and referral tool, not unlicensed legal advice.
- **Willingness to pay:** diaspora willingness to pay is untested.

The build score is 4 rather than 5 because there are two components to finish over the weekend.

### Pasoš: a voice-note spray log that becomes an EU residue passport

**Pitch:** pepper and cucumber growers log sprays by voice message in Macedonian. Pasoš answers with the safe-harvest date, and exporters receive a QR passport for every batch, so loads stop being destroyed at the Bulgarian border.

**Problem evidence.** Bulgaria's food agency blocked and ordered the destruction of **9.5 tonnes** of Macedonian peppers over pesticide residues ([FreshPlaza](https://www.freshplaza.com/article/9871656/bulgaria-blocks-28-5-tons-of-grapes-and-peppers-over-pesticide-levels/)). Croatia withdrew peppers carrying methomyl at 0.049 mg/kg against a 0.01 limit ([HortiDaily](https://www.hortidaily.com/article/9772414/macedonian-peppers-withdrawn-from-croatian-market-due-to-contamination)). National monitoring found cucumbers carried the most residues, mainly methomyl ([UGD eprints](https://eprints.ugd.edu.mk/14026/)). The EU's default limit of 0.01 mg/kg under Reg. 396/2005 leaves little room for error ([Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/record/4458711)).

**Competitors.** GlobalG.A.P.-style farm systems are built for large farms, not 1.8 ha smallholders. Regional platforms such as Seljak.me and Farmer.ba are marketplaces, not compliance tools ([FAO Agritech Observatory](https://agritechobservatory.review.fao.org/es/seljakme)).

**Who pays.** The proposed model has three revenue lines:

- **Exporters and aggregators, the main payer:** about €3–5 per supplying farmer per season-month, or a per-batch passport fee. They are the ones whose consignments get destroyed, and one exporter onboards hundreds of growers. That is a clean B2B2C channel for the marketing section of the video.
- **Agro-pharmacies:** a possible second line, for verified product data. This needs a firewall against product promotion.
- **Donors and the Food and Veterinary Agency:** a third line, funding traceability pilots.

**Weekend MVP.** A Telegram or WhatsApp-style bot does speech-to-text and LLM parsing of "sprayed product X on peppers today." It looks the product up in a seeded table of active ingredients, EU limits and label pre-harvest intervals, then returns the safe-harvest date. An exporter dashboard flags at-risk batches, and a public QR page serves as the batch passport.

**Wow moment.** A team member sends a real Macedonian voice note on stage. The bot replies "safe to harvest from 14 October." A judge then scans the passport on a mock crate.

**Risks.**

- **Self-reported data is not proof.** The passport must be paired with sample lab tests to be credible.
- **Farmer adoption:** growers must actually log, so the incentive has to come from the exporter.
- **Reference data:** building the label-interval table takes agronomist time.
- **Dated evidence:** interception reports are undated in the sources.

The idea scales well beyond North Macedonia. Every non-EU fresh-produce exporter faces the same limits. That makes it the strongest pure "business" idea in the set and the safest clean break from Niva.

### Toplo: the stove-switch calculator that pays for itself

**Pitch:** enter your address and how you heat. Toplo shows what wood, an inverter AC or pellets cost you and your street this winter, finds the subsidy or green loan that covers the switch, and pre-fills the application.

**Problem evidence.** It is the strongest and most visceral in the set. Skopje has been ranked Europe's most polluted capital, 40% of households pick heating by the bill, and a third admit they or their neighbours burn waste ([UNDP](https://www.undp.org/north-macedonia/news/major-new-survey-household-heating-identifies-some-key-causes-skopjes-soaring-levels-pollution); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/gallery/2021/4/21/in-pictures-air-pollution-in-north-macedonia)).

**Funding exists.** EBRD's GEFF facility has reached more than 2,000 households with up to 20% cashback ([EBRD](https://www.ebrd.com/home/news-and-events/news/2021/ebrd-and-partners-support-residential-energy-efficiency-in-north-macedonia.html)). The Ministry of Energy's 2025 programme gives free inverter ACs to low-income households ([Balkan Green Energy News](https://balkangreenenergynews.com/?p=84504)).

**Competitors.** AirCare and Pulse.eco measure pollution; nobody solves the household's switch decision ([Wikipedia: Gorjan Jovanovski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorjan_Jovanovski)).

**Who pays.** Three payers are proposed:

- **GEFF partner banks:** cost per qualified loan lead.
- **Installers:** about 5% commission.
- **The City of Skopje:** programme-administration software, since it once received 4,282 applications for a single scheme ([Balkan Green Energy News](https://balkangreenenergynews.com/?p=35771)).

**Weekend MVP.** Open-Meteo heating degree-days, a cost and emissions model for each heating option, rule-based subsidy matching, an LLM-filled application, and a city map of PM2.5 avoided.

**Risks and score.** The evidence cuts against the core premise. Those 4,282 applications were suspended when funds ran out, and up to 70% of households say they would not take a loan to replace their stove ([Meta.mk](https://meta.mk/en/solar-thermal-collectors-could-replace-wood-burning-stoves-help-fight-air-pollution-and-could-provide-for-cheaper-electricity/)). The binding constraint may be money, not navigation. Energy calculators also sit uncomfortably close to the saturated carbon-tracker category. Hence the 3s on Solution and Business. Toplo would be the best pick for a team that wants the most emotional, data-rich video and is willing to defend the business model in Q&A.

### Blizu: a trusted pair of hands for parents left behind

**Pitch:** children abroad book and pay, in euros, for vetted local helpers who visit, shop, collect medicine and send a photo check-in to the family app. The parent only needs a one-button phone.

**Problem evidence.** 458 villages have under 50 residents ([OBC Transeuropa](https://www.balcanicaucaso.org/en/cp_article/the-death-of-macedonian-village/)). Rural care access is uneven, and families rely on unpaid care, mostly by women ([UN Women 2026](https://eca.unwomen.org/en/digital-library/publications/2026/05/transforming-care-systems-in-north-macedonia-gender-care-needs-and-financing-pathways-for-an-inclusive-care-economy)). The Red Cross emergency-button pilot covers only about 50–60 clients ([Red Cross MK](https://ckrm.org.mk/en/integrated-care-and-support-service-business-development-in-the-red-cross-of-republic-of-north-macedonia-financed-by-the-austrian-development-agency-ada-austrian-red-cross-and-swiss-red-cross)). A World Bank tender to assess the private long-term-care market signals that the market is still immature ([World Bank procurement](https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/corporate-procurement/business-opportunities/administrative-procurement/rfxnow-2002166-private-long-term-care-sector-assessment-north-macedonia)).

**Who pays.** The payer is the clearest in the set: diaspora children earning EU incomes, on a proposed €29–49 monthly plan with a 20–30% take rate on helper hours. Municipalities, which carry legal responsibility under the 2019 social-protection law, are a later B2G line ([InCARE](https://incare.euro.centre.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/InCARE-Policy-Roadmap-North-Macedonia_final.pdf)).

**Weekend MVP and wow.** The build is a family app with booking and test-mode payment, a helper app with photo check-in, and an SMS or one-button check-in for the parent. The wow moment is a live check-in photo from "Grandma in Kičevo" landing on a phone in Vilnius.

**Risks.** The value lives in operations: vetting helpers, liability and keeping the service reliable in remote villages. Home-care marketplaces are a familiar model elsewhere, so the Innovation score is modest. The story score is the highest possible, because almost every family in the region knows this situation.

### Hlad: heat-stress scheduling for the days that aren't heatwaves

**Pitch:** Hlad turns tomorrow's hourly forecast into a work-rest schedule for each site and sends alerts to field crews and construction teams. It is aimed at ordinary hot days, not just official heatwaves.

**Problem evidence.** The ILO counts **2.41 billion workers** exposed to excessive heat, with 22.85 million injuries and 18,970 deaths a year. Nine in ten exposures and eight in ten injuries happen **outside** heatwaves ([ILO](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/more-workers-ever-are-losing-fight-against-heat-stress)). North Macedonia declared a 30-day crisis during the 2024 heatwave and wildfires ([ANSA](https://ansa.it/nuova_europa/en/news/countries/macedonia/2024/07/14/wild-fires-and-heatwave-n.-macedonia-proclaims-state-of-crises_8f52b2e1-59fe-47ee-9a66-d7fe373806f4.html)), and about 24% of the labour force works in agriculture ([UKLO eprints](https://eprints.uklo.edu.mk/10208/1/Pashovska%20Silvana%2C%20Intewrnational%20conference%20NIsh%202021.docx)).

**Build.** This is the easiest build in the set. Open-Meteo hourly temperature, humidity, wind and radiation feed a WBGT approximation (an index that combines heat, humidity and sun), which drives a rule engine and SMS alerts.

**Who pays and risks.** Proposed payers are construction and agri employers on a per-worker monthly plan, plus insurers. The weak points are willingness to pay among informal employers and thin North Macedonia-specific injury data. For an international jury it travels well, since every country has outdoor workers.

### Izvor: find the 60% of water that never gets billed

**Pitch:** a small utility uploads its billing export and bulk-meter readings. Izvor builds the IWA water balance (the standard accounting of where supplied water goes) for each zone and ranks the accounts and districts losing the most water.

**Problem evidence.** It is the most rigorous in the set: national non-revenue water near 62%, Bitola at about 59%, and Skopje-region losses above 60% ([Meta.mk](https://meta.mk/en/?p=556300); [State Audit Office](https://arhiva.dzr.mk/en/node/3848); [BTA](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/balkans/932853-skopje-experiencing-water-supply-problems)). It also sits in an under-crowded goal, SDG 6.

**Competitors.** ASTERRA and FIDO sell sensors and satellite services to larger utilities ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260128042023/en/New-Mexico-Expands-State-Funded-Satellite-Leak-Detection-Program-Following-Breakthrough-Success-in-Truth-or-Concequences)). Izvor would be the cheap, software-only triage layer below them.

**Business and risks.** Proposed revenue is SaaS per connection per year, with donor-funded pilots through EBRD, KfW or SECO. B2G sales cycles are slow, the demo needs synthetic data modelled on the audit findings, and a dashboard is a dry video. Judges have also just seen water win Hult with hardware.

### Javno: an AI reader for tenders built to have one bidder

**Pitch:** Javno reads tender documents and flags restrictive criteria and suspiciously short deadlines. It sells the same analysis to SMEs as alerts for "tenders you can actually win."

**Problem evidence.** **42% of EU contracts in 2021 had a single bidder**, and average bidders fell from 5.7 to 3.2 over a decade ([ECA](https://www.eca.europa.eu/ECAHTMLNews/NEWS-SR-2023-28/en/body.html)). Existing tools are rule-based and NGO-run ([TI EU](https://transparency.eu/integrity-watch-red-flags)). SDG 16 is rarely pitched.

**Risks.** The weakest payer in the set. It is also uncertain whether North Macedonia's national e-procurement portal can be accessed openly for a demo, since the TED API covers EU notices ([TED API](https://docs.ted.europa.eu/api/latest/index.html)).

### Ideas considered and dropped

Several other candidates were dropped:

- **An EUDR plot passport:** strong timing, with obligations applying from 30 Dec 2026 ([Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/18/deforestation-council-signs-off-targeted-revision-to-simplify-and-postpone-the-regulation/)). But funded incumbents are crowding in, and a Skopje team has no proximity to coffee or cocoa farmers.
- **A CBAM data kit for Balkan exporters:** a real cost, about €262m on North Macedonia's power exports on a 2023 basis ([Balkan Green Energy News](https://balkangreenenergynews.com/bruegel-without-refining-or-delaying-cbam-for-electricity-eu-risks-market-integration-security-of-supply/)), but no human story.
- **Pharmacy stock finders:** pharmacy data access is uncertain.
- **Wildfire volunteer coordination:** seasonal, with a B2G-only payer.
- **Illegal-dump reporting:** citizen-reporting apps already exist.
- **Remittances:** a fintech-crowded space.
- **A standalone women's inheritance navigator:** strongest when absorbed into Udel.

## Pitch Udel, and film it as Niva's second act

Udel, Pasoš and Toplo tie on points. Three tie-breakers decide it for Udel.

**First, the deadline.** About 60 hours remain. Udel reuses Niva's research, statistics, satellite visuals and narrative, while a clean switch to Pasoš means rebuilding evidence from scratch.

**Second, Udel turns the team's most dangerous finding into its sharpest insight.** "Everyone thinks the problem is finding abandoned land. The real problem is that the law needs every heir's signature, and they live in four countries." Juries reward exactly this kind of counter-intuitive, locally grounded insight. It also answers the Q&A question that would otherwise sink Niva.

**Third, Udel has the best fit with Creative Shock's emphasis on marketing and finance.** Marketing: a free satellite report is a hook that diaspora groups will share. Finance: the fee is priced on the value of the land, not on a tiny rent.

Choose **Pasoš** instead if a quick call with a lawyer reveals that an amendment allows majority leasing of agricultural land. Choose it too if the team cannot get even three diaspora heirs to confirm the pain before filming. Pasoš has the cleaner business model and no legal dependency.

## Conclusion

The research changes what "originality" means for this competition. The team does not need an unseen problem. It needs an unseen mechanism inside a well-documented problem. Abandoned farmland is well known, and FAO, the EU and the Ministry all talk about it. Nobody has built the thing that breaks the specific legal and family deadlock behind it. The same pattern runs through the strongest alternatives. Pesticide rejections are known, but nobody has made logging as easy as a voice note. Smog is known, but nobody has solved the household's decision of what to switch to and who pays. The ideas that beat Niva did not find bigger problems. They found the choke point where a small actor holds the data and a larger actor will pay to get it.

The second takeaway is about evidence, not ideas. Every top-ranked idea has its weakest score in Business & Sustainability, because no source anywhere tested willingness to pay. Over the next 48 hours, the most valuable work for any choice is three to five recorded conversations with real payers. Those conversations count twice: they provide the user-testing evidence that Hult-style judges explicitly look for, and they are the line in the finance section that a Vilnius jury of business academics will find hardest to dismiss.
