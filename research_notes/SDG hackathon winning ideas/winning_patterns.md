# What wins SDG / social-impact business idea competitions and student hackathons (2019-2026)

Research date: 2026-10-09. Scope: Creative Shock (ISM, Vilnius), Hult Prize, ClimateLaunchpad, Enactus World Cup, EIT Food student challenges, Social Impact Award, Junction, and assorted SDG hackathons. Every cited line has a URL. Inferences are labelled as such. Material older than 2019 is flagged.

## 1. Creative Shock (ISM University, Vilnius): recent placings, ideas, 2026 format, judging

### Takeaway
Up to 2025, Creative Shock was a **social-business case competition**: teams solved consulting cases for real Lithuanian social enterprises, so results reflect consulting skill more than original ideas. **2026 rebrands it as an "International Impact Hackathon"**: Round 1 is a 3-5 minute video pitch of an *original* impact business idea tied to one SDG, and the Top 10 then "develop, refine, and present" their solution in Vilnius. I found no official source that mentions vibe-coding or a required MVP, and no published rubric. The only stated focus is "marketing and finance".

### Cited Findings
**2026 format (current edition)**
- Creative Shock 2026 calls itself an "International Impact Hackathon". It is open to **Bachelor students in teams of 3-4**. — [Creative Shock 2026 event description PDF (via HS Stralsund)](https://www.hochschule-stralsund.de/storages/hs-stralsund/International/Outgoing/News__Events__Scholarships/Creative_Shock_event_description.pdf)
- **First Round:** "Teams submit a 3–5 minute video pitch presenting an original impact business idea aligned with one of the 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals." The strongest teams advance to Vilnius. — [same PDF](https://www.hochschule-stralsund.de/storages/hs-stralsund/International/Outgoing/News__Events__Scholarships/Creative_Shock_event_description.pdf)
- **Second Round (Final Weekend in Vilnius):** workshops, mentorship from industry professionals, "Develop, refine, and present your solution to an international jury", plus networking and cultural activities. The PDF does not mention coding, an MVP or AI tools. — [same PDF](https://www.hochschule-stralsund.de/storages/hs-stralsund/International/Outgoing/News__Events__Scholarships/Creative_Shock_event_description.pdf)
- Official site: registration opens Sept 1, **online qualification round Oct 5-11**, and the **TOP 10 Final Weekend runs Nov 18-21 (Vilnius)**. The pitch is 3-5 minutes on an "original impact business idea" aligned with the "UN 17 goals", with a **"marketing and finance" focus**. The final weekend includes "building, workshops, mentoring, a conference, and a cultural night". Winners get a **$4000 package for the TOP 3**, plus founder support and mentoring. The first-round task is sent by email, so check spam. — [creativeshock.lt](https://creativeshock.lt/)
- Site discrepancy: the FAQ still shows 2025 details (a 100 EUR per-person finalist fee and 3000/1000/500 EUR prizes). — [creativeshock.lt](https://creativeshock.lt/)
- Partner-university call: participation is free, registration runs **Sept 1 to Oct 4, 2026**, online qualifications Oct 5-11, hotel and meals are covered for the Top 10, and the winning team gets a USD 4,000 prize fund. — [UGD (North Macedonia) call](https://fi.ugd.edu.mk/en/call-for-participation-in-the-international-creative-shock-2026-hackathon/)
- No source mentions "vibe-coding". A targeted search returned only generic vibe-coding articles. — (search, see Gaps)

- **2nd: University of Belgrade FON** (Tamara Kukić, Ana Milivojević, Nikola Černicin; members of the Case Study Club / BCA SEE). FON's report describes two cases:

**Earlier editions (case-based)**
- **10th edition (reported Dec 2020): Ateneo de Manila "Big Blue Energy" won**, out of 290 teams from 85 countries. - The Case Centre keeps a Creative Shock results page but returned HTTP 403. — [Case Centre](https://www.thecasecentre.org/caseLearning/competitionResults/CreativeShock)

### Inferences
- Past Creative Shock juries rewarded **locally grounded, implementable, financially sustainable** answers for small social enterprises (disability employment, migrant integration, rural revenue). The 2026 jury will likely bring the same lens to original ideas: favour concrete, small-scale, financeable models over "global platform" ideas.
- The stated "marketing and finance" focus suggests the 3-5 min video must explicitly cover **customer acquisition (marketing) and revenue/unit economics (finance)**, not just the SDG problem.
- The words "hackathon" and "building" on the 2026 final weekend suggest live prototype work. Having a clickable or working prototype ready to iterate on is likely an advantage, but this is **not confirmed** by any official document.
- The Rotman precedent (a homelessness idea for their own city) and the UGM quote both point to **local, context-specific framing** as a recurring winning trait at this competition.

### Gaps
- 2022, 2023 and 2024 winners and placings: not found (Case Centre blocked; no university press release surfaced).
- No published judging rubric or past jury feedback for any edition.
- No official mention of vibe-coding or MVP requirements for 2026. The final-weekend "building" detail is the only hint.
- Specific solutions from the 2025 1st (UGM) and 2nd (FON) teams are not published.

**Official selection framework**
- The scorecard is progressive. **Product-Market Fit and Go-To-Market** are introduced at Nationals. **Traction** ("successful execution of pilot projects, revenue generation, customer acquisition, and developing strategic partnerships") comes at the Accelerator/Incubator stage. **Scaling** (replicating in new markets, economies of scale) comes at Finals. — [Hult Prize selection criteria](https://www.hultprize.org/how-it-works/selection-process-criteria)
- Judges score individually, then deliberate, and may also weigh diversity of region, industry and stage when shaping the finalist pool. — [Hult Prize selection criteria](https://www.hultprize.org/how-it-works/selection-process-criteria)
- Campus-level rubric (HKUST; may differ from the global scorecard): **Team 25%** (complementary skills, roles, pitch and Q&A), **Idea 25%** (problem understanding, innovation, "**tested with real users** and feedback integrated"), **Impact 25%** (SDG alignment, **clear impact KPIs**, impact that grows with revenue), and **Business Viability 25%** (business model, **unit economics**, path to profit, competitive advantage). — [HKUST Hult Prize page](https://ec.hkust.edu.hk/node/11978)
- Stages: Qualifiers (Sept-Feb), Nationals (Apr-May), Digital Incubator (Jun-Jul; build POCs and MVPs), Global Accelerator (Aug, Ashridge), Global Finals (Sept). — [hultprize.org/scorecard](https://www.hultprize.org/scorecard)

### Inferences
- **Four of the five 2022-2026 single winners are physical products, and the fifth is hardware plus software.** None is a pure app. Each pairs tech with a cheap physical artefact and a named low-income or under-served user. Pure-software finalists in 2026 (Capynova, Dihadi, CleanUp MDC) did not win.
- Winners were usually **years old** (Stick 'Em ~5 years) and had pilots or contracts. At the global level, Hult rewards traction more than idea novelty.
- Health (SDG 3) is heavily represented among Hult finalists: 4 of the 8 2026 finalists plus the 2024 winner.

## 4. What judges say they reward or penalize, and what makes a 3-5 min video pitch win

### Takeaway
Official rubrics reward these, in this order:
1. A deep understanding of a specific problem.
2. A solution **tested with real users**.
3. **Measurable impact KPIs** that grow with revenue.
4. Clear unit economics and a go-to-market plan.
5. A credible, complementary team.

Known penalties come mostly from general impact and investor guidance, not competition-published statements: SDG-tagging without concrete targets, no evidence of a paying customer, grant dependence, and vague, huge markets. For short video pitches the best evidence is a hook, a founder or customer story, a short demo of the user flow, and an explicit business model.

### Cited Findings
- Hult (campus rubric): Idea criteria include "Has the solution been tested with real users and has feedback been integrated?". Impact criteria include "Are there clear KPIs measuring impact? ... impact growth with increased revenue". Viability covers unit economics and a realistic path to profitability. — [HKUST Hult Prize](https://ec.hkust.edu.hk/node/11978)
- Hult (official): traction means pilots, revenue, customer acquisition and partnerships; scaling means replicating in new markets. — [Hult Prize selection criteria](https://www.hultprize.org/how-it-works/selection-process-criteria)
- Hult 2026 winner rationale: "turning an urgent global problem into a practical, scalable business." — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/1-million-awarded-to-social-impact-student-start-up-at-2026-ef-hult-prize-302872716.html)

### Inferences
- A strong 3-5 min SDG video likely follows this order:
  1. **A 15-second hook** with a local, specific statistic or a person's story.
  2. **Why us**: proximity to the problem.
  3. **The specific customer and who pays.**
  4. **A 30-60 second demo** of the prototype's core flow.
  5. **The business model and unit economics** (Creative Shock's "finance").
  6. **Go-to-market or first 100 customers** (Creative Shock's "marketing").
  7. **One SDG target with 1-2 measurable impact KPIs.**
  8. **The ask or next step.**
- To avoid looking "app-for-everything", name one SDG and target rather than five. Show evidence such as interviews, a waitlist or a pilot letter. Explain why the solution fits the local context.
- Creative Shock 2026 limits entry to Bachelor teams and has a short timeline: registration closed Oct 4, and the round ran Oct 5-11. The bar is therefore likely a well-reasoned idea with a believable prototype, not Hult-level traction.

### Gaps
- No competition published jury feedback on what was penalized. The "SDG-washing" and "no customer" penalties come from general guidance, not competition statements.
- No empirical study (only advice) was found on what makes a video-pitch qualification round succeed.
- No Creative Shock video-round rubric was found.
